The **representation** is a central component of theories in cognitive science and neuroscience. A recent tred has to been to try and understand a *representational geometry* or *topology* – a latent space where points reflect a particular item within a to-be-understood set, and the distance between those items reflect a psychological distance. For example, the items could be exemplars of a particular set (e.g. fruits) and the geometry of the representation would reflect a layout where the distances between the fruits reflect some psychological distance that can then be used to predict cognitive behaviours or differences in neural populations.
