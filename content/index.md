title: Welcome to my public brain!
---
You have discovered my [digital garden](https://fabric.so/learn/digital-garden) – a place for me to store and grow my ideas. I started this project as a small resistance to artificial intelligence; I wanted to have a space where I could put down my thinking and watch it expand and evolve as I put more into it. I also liked the idea of a public forum where my developing thoughts could be viewed by passersby, who might appreciate the ideas as they visited.

I am a passionate cognitive scientist studying how we select and represent information in our mind and in our brain – our **attention** and **working memory**. This garden is populated directly from my notes within an [Obsidian vault](https://obsidian.md/) using [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/); I want it to be both a reflection of what I have read and learned, and a live snapshot of what I am seeking to understand and have in my mind. This reflects the inspiration for the name of this garden, *long-term working memory*, which comes from [Ericsson and Kintsch (1995)](https://psycnet.apa.org/doi/10.1037/0033-295X.102.2.211) who argued that long-term memories could be made directly accessible via working memory. I hope this digital garden becomes a long-term and active store my research career.

The organisation of this garden is somewhat hierarchical, loosely based on the *Theory Construction Methodology* of [Borsboom, van der Mass and Haig (2021)]([https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691620969647](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691620969647)). There are phenomena that I seek to understand (concepts), ways that those phenomena are explained or explored by the field (models), and those explanations are supported by various sources of evidence (my own research projects, papers I have read, talks I have watched, and other miscellaneous notes).  

```
├── 📂 concepts                # GitHub configuration and CI/CD pipelines
│   └── 📂 workflows           # GitHub Actions workflow definitions
├── 📂 models                   # Configuration files (environment, database, app)
├── 📂 projects                 # Documentation, architecture, and user guides
│   └── 📂 ideas                # GitHub Actions workflow definitions
│   └── 📂 in_progress          # GitHub Actions workflow definitions
│   └── 📂 complete
├── 📂 papers                   # Core application source code
├── 📂 talks                    # Unit, integration, and end-to-end tests
└── index.md                    # The home page you are viewing
```
